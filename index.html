    Questions

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How Do You Offer Fares Below the Airline’s Published Price?

    We negotiate fares directly with our partner airlines. These contract rates are not published online, so the fare we quote you can sit below the price on the airline’s own website.

    Airlines also price their seats dynamically. The same Business Class seat can sell at several fare levels, depending on inventory and timing, and our specialists follow those levels on your route to quote you at the right moment.

    You fly the same seat, on the same flight, for less.

    How Quickly Will I Hear Back?

    A consultant replies within 24 hours with fares from our partner airlines, by email or phone, whichever you prefer.

    How Do I Buy Business Class Tickets Through You?

    Request a free quote with your route and dates. Your consultant replies within 24 hours with fares from our partner airlines and, once you choose one, books your tickets for you. Nothing is booked until you confirm.

    Are There Any Booking Fees?

    No. Requesting a quote is free and carries no obligation, and we do not add booking fees to your fare.

    Can I Change My Dates or Cancel?

    Your ticket is valid for one year. You may rebook for other dates within that year, or change your departure and arrival cities within the same continent, subject to availability. Tickets are non-refundable, but if you cancel, the amount you paid is held for your future travel.

    Why Don’t You List Every Airline and Route?

    To avoid competing with our airline partners’ own sales, we do not publish every partner or route on this website. If you do not see your journey, request a quote and we will find it.

    Do You Book for Groups and Companies?

    Yes. For more than nine passengers, your consultant will arrange a group fare. Companies can also open a corporate account with a dedicated account manager and one monthly invoice.
