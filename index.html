Why it costs less

Airlines don't sell every business seat at the price you see online. A share of each cabin goes to consolidators at negotiated rates the airline agrees never to publish, so it can fill the cabin without lowering its public fare.

Those fares are real tickets on the same flights, but an agency can only offer them on request. We compare them with the published fares for your dates and send you whichever is lower.

Questions, answered

Is it the same ticket the airline sells? Yes. Your ticket is issued by the airline, appears in its app under your booking reference, and gets the same seat, lounge and baggage as any other business ticket.

Will I earn miles and status? In most cases, yes. Accrual depends on the booking class, which your advisor tells you before you pay.

Why can't I just book online? Consolidator fares are sold under contracts that keep them out of public searches. A short request is how we can show them to you.

How quickly will I hear back? Usually within the hour, by email or text, whichever you left us. For travel in the next two days, please call.

Can I change or cancel? That depends on the fare. Many allow date changes for the difference in fare; your advisor shows the rules for each option before you choose.

How do I pay? By card through a secure payment link, once you've chosen an itinerary. Nothing is charged when you send a request.

Do you sell first class and premium economy? Yes. Choose the cabin in the form, or ask for both and compare.

Are prices really in dollars? Always. Every fare we quote is in US dollars per person with taxes and fees included.
