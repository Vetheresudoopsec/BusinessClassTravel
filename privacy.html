Privacy Policy

This policy sets out what this site collects, why, and who else receives it.

The terms that apply to quote requests are in our Terms of Service.

Last updated 4 November 2026

1. Details You Give Us

1.1When you request a quote, we collect what you enter in the booking form: your first and last name, email address, phone number and its country, your route, dates, whether they are flexible, the number of passengers, the cabin and any other flights.

If you add details on the thank-you page, such as preferred airlines or frequent flyer numbers, we collect those too.

1.2The corporate account form collects your company name, work email address and number of travellers. The contact form collects your name, your email address or phone number, and your itinerary.

1.3We also record the time you sent the form and the page you sent it from.

1.4These details are emailed to our team so a consultant can prepare your quote. They are not kept on the website itself.

When you request a quote, we also email you a confirmation of the trip you asked about.

We keep these emails for as long as we need them to prepare your quote, arrange your travel and keep our business records.

2. Your Approximate Location

2.1To suggest your departure airport, your phone country code and the fare offers shown to you, the site looks up your approximate area from your IP address.

The lookup uses location details provided by Cloudflare where available, and otherwise one of these services: ipapi.co, ipinfo.io or geojs.io. The service receives your IP address.

2.2We keep the result (country, city and approximate coordinates) on our server for up to one day, under a scrambled (hashed) copy of your IP address rather than the address itself, so a repeat visit does not need a new lookup.

3. Advertising and Analytics Cookies

3.1This site uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads. They load when you visit and set cookies in your browser.

They send Google information about your visit, such as the pages you view, the ad you came from, your browser and device, and your IP address.

3.2We use this information to understand how visitors use the site and to measure how well our advertising works, including how many visitors request a quote after clicking one of our ads.

Google may process this information outside your own country. Please see Google’s privacy policy for details.

3.3You can limit this at any time:

  • manage the ads Google shows you at adssettings.google.com
  • install Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on
  • block or delete cookies in your browser settings

We do not sell your information.

4. Browser Storage

4.1The site saves some details in your browser so it works as you expect. These hold:

  • the trip you are building in the booking form (route, dates, passengers, cabin and whether your dates are flexible), so it is still there when you return. This holds no name, email address or phone number.
  • the request you have just sent (your name, email address and trip), so the thank-you page can show it back to you. This is kept in session storage and cleared when you close the tab.

These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

5. Other Services We Use

5.1Our hosting provider’s Cloudflare network delivers this site. It may run a security check before showing a page, sets cookies to remember that you passed, and receives your IP address and browser details.

5.2Some destination photos are loaded from Unsplash and Picsum. These services receive your IP address and browser details when your browser loads them.

5.3Our hosting provider’s email service delivers your requests to us and your confirmation to you.

6. Protecting Against Misuse

6.1To keep automated programs from flooding our inbox, our server counts how many requests come from each visitor. It keeps a scrambled (hashed) copy of your IP address and the times of your requests for 10 minutes.

The forms also include a hidden field that people never see. Requests that fill it in are discarded.

7. Sharing Your Details

7.1When you choose to book, we share the passenger details needed to issue your tickets with the airline.

We do not share your details with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

8. Your Choices

8.1To see the details we hold about you, correct them or have them deleted, email us with the name and email address you used.
