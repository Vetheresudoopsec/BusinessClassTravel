Questions

Is it the same ticket the airline sells?

Yes. The airline issues your ticket, it appears in the airline's app under your booking reference, and you get the same seat, lounge and baggage allowance.

Will I earn miles?

In most cases, yes. Your advisor tells you the booking class, and what it earns, before you pay.

Why can't I book online?

These fares are sold under contracts that keep them out of public searches, so an advisor prices each trip by hand.

What currency are quotes in?

Every quote is in US dollars per person, with taxes and fees included.

Is my request binding?

No. Nothing is charged until you choose an itinerary and pay through a secure link.
