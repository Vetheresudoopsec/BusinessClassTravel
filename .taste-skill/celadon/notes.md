# Celadon, refined — five directions (2026-10-21)

Brief: concept 1 (Celadon) made more modern and tasteful. Standing constraints from the client: booking block visible on
opening, light and comfortable palette, no routes or prices, each block in its own space, transparent booking, Asia Pacific luxury.
Palette kept (--keep palette): porcelain #F6F8F6, white, celadon tint #E3EEE9, celadon #CBDDD4, ink #17231F, soft ink #4F5E58,
accent celadon green #2F6B5A, deep celadon #1E3A32.

## Inventory
- Name: BusinessClassTravel — three plain words for exactly what is sold.
- Constraint: agency fares can't be shown publicly; every price is given on request, by a person, in writing.
- Output: a written quote with two or three options (flights, seat, rules, total).
- One advisor from request to return.
- Asia Pacific: Hong Kong harbour, Changi (Jewel, T3 ceiling), dawn above the cloud deck.
- The request itself is one sentence: who, which cabin, from where, to where, when.
- Consolidation: many airlines' fares gathered and reduced to a few options.

## Directions (dealt with --keep palette, tone luxury)
A. sentence-as-layout — type redrawn (Anton caps → Bricolage Grotesque): heavy condensed caps fight the calm luxury brief.
   Idea: the traveller's request, one sentence, becomes the sentence the page is set in; the booking form is written as that
   sentence with fields where the words go. Live thing: the sentence form.
B. type-only-statement (Archivo). Idea: "by request" — the constraint — becomes the seam; the headline crosses from paper into
   the celadon field where the request form waits.
C. place-carries-it (Boska → Instrument Serif/Sans: Boska is Fontshare-only and the canvas loads Google Fonts only).
   Replaces loud-flat-poster (flat saturated poster contradicts the light, calm brief). Idea: the view from the window seat at
   dawn opens the page; each step of booking is a moment in a drag row.
D. editorial-report (Schibsted Grotesk). Idea: consolidation — many fares gathered, reduced, handed back as a few options —
   becomes the many-into-one diagram.
E. name-as-monument (Bodoni Moda/Jost). Idea: the name, which says exactly what is sold, becomes the moving line.

Adjustment on every hero: the booking form is placed in the first screen (client constraint), where the blueprint had
picture or empty ground.
